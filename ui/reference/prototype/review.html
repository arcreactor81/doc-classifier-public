What belongs here

What doesn't belong here
Instructions the reader follows step by step; those belong in Procedures. names ProceduresRecords of what happened over a period belong in Reports. names Reports
Examples (3)
  • Slides introducing a new policy
  • A handout on how budgets are set
  • An overview of the year's plans

Folder: explainers

Reports
What belongs here
Documents that record what happened or what was found over a period: summaries, reviews and results.
What doesn't belong here
Plans for future work, and slides that explain a topic; those belong in Explainers. names Explainers
Examples (3)
  • A quarterly summary
  • An annual review
  • Survey results

Folder: reports

Forms

What doesn't belong here
