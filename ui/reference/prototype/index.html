Screens

Each opens in both themes; use the round button at the top right to switch.

  • Home Runs that need you, as links. Nothing is continued from here.
  • Confirm Choose how to run it and set a spending limit. Nothing is pre-selected.
  • Progress: sorting 73 of 114 decided, with the pipeline track. Try the motion here.
  • Progress: sending stopped Stopped at 13 of 114, with what it means and Continue sending.
  • Results A compact table with outcome pills and one document's evidence open.
  • Review folders The checklist beside the categories the run used. Narrow the window to see the snapped layout.
  • System Setup, filing certainty and the emergency stop, with its confirmation sheet.

What to look at

  • Surfaces. Soft steps between the page, the cards and inset panes, with hairline borders. Pure white is kept for sheets only.
  • One accent. Blue means "you are here" and "you can act here". Green, amber and red mean only Filed, Review and Could not process.
  • Type. The system face of this computer, in three weights; big numbers and headings in its display cut.
  • One motion motif. A thin light trail runs through the journey and into every progress track. It moves only when something is recorded. Try it on Progress.
  • Reduced motion. With the reduce-motion setting on, every state, word and number stays the same; only the movement goes.

Where the motion is

Every movement is tied to a recorded event and lasts 5 seconds or less

  • Progress. Select "1 more decided": the pipeline boundaries move, the changed counts flash once, the new activity line fades in, and one trail sweep runs along Decided (at most once every 5 seconds). At 114 the journey advances in place and the button beneath turns into See the results.
  • Sending stopped. Everything is still. Select Continue sending: the track turns from dashed to moving and fills to the next recorded count.
  • Results. The 2 px indicator slides under the chosen filter.
  • System. The confirmation sheet fades in over the page.
  • Never: count-up numbers, bounce, pulse, rotation, hover lift, or a theme cross-fade. The theme switch and the focus ring are instant.
